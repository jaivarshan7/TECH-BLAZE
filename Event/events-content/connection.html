Connection

Welcome to Connections! Put your thinking to the test as you decode hidden links between seemingly unrelated images. This isn’t just about what you see; it’s about how you think. Connect the clues, find the logic, and uncover the common thread before the clock runs out. Let the brain games begin!

Description

Connection is a fun and fast-paced game that tests how quickly you can link images and find the word. In this game, you will see a set of different images that might look like they have nothing in common at first. Your goal is to use logic to find the hidden link between them and guess the correct word, person, or place they represent. The game gets more challenging as you move through the Easy, Medium, and Hard levels, starting with simple word combinations and moving toward complex ideas. It is the perfect way to show off your creativity, observation skills, and quick thinking!

Event Structure
  • Participation: Maximum of 2-4 participants per team.
General Rules
  • Time Limit: Participants face a strictly timed challenge for every slide.
  • Progression: The game gets more difficult as you move from Round 1 to Round 3.
  • Negative Marking: Be careful with your answers! Every incorrect guess will result in a deduction of points.
  • No External Help: The use of mobile phones or the internet is strictly prohibited.
Rounds

Round 1: The Debug Log (Easy)

  • Difficulty: Low. The connections are literal and simple.
  • Format: You will see images that join together to form basic compound words or common names.
  • Scoring: +5 points for each correct answer.

Round 2: The Link Layer (Medium)

  • Difficulty: Moderate. The connections are more abstract.
  • Format: You will see a set of clues that belong to a specific category or theme. You must identify the link and justify your logic.
  • Scoring: +10 points for each correct answer.

Round 3: The Core Connection (Hard)

  • Difficulty: High. The connections require deep general knowledge and reverse reasoning.
  • Format: You will see multiple layered sets of images representing complex behaviors or systems. No single image gives away the answer easily.
  • Scoring: +20 points for each correct answer.
